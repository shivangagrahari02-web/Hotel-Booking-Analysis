Hotel Booking & Cancellation Analysis

Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end exploratory data analysis case study focused on hotel booking behavior and cancellation trends. The analysis was performed using Python in Jupyter Notebook to uncover factors influencing hotel booking cancellations, customer behavior, seasonal demand, and revenue-related patterns. The project helps hotels understand customer booking habits and identify strategies to reduce cancellations and optimize occupancy rates.

Project Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze hotel booking data to identify the major factors contributing to booking cancellations and customer behavior patterns. The analysis aimed to help hotels improve operational efficiency, optimize pricing strategies, reduce revenue loss from cancellations, and enhance customer retention.

Business Problem Statements

1. Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis

Identify the major reasons and patterns behind hotel booking cancellations to help hotels minimize revenue leakage and improve occupancy planning.

2. Customer Behavior Analysis

Analyze customer booking preferences such as hotel type, lead time, market segment, stay duration, and seasonal trends to understand customer decision-making patterns.

3. Revenue & Demand Trend Analysis

Study booking trends across months and customer segments to identify peak seasons, high-demand periods, and revenue opportunities.

4. Booking Channel Performance

Evaluate the effectiveness of different booking channels and market segments in terms of confirmed bookings and cancellation probability.
